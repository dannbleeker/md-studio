# Writing as Practice

### Plain text as a discipline, not a limitation

> **Draft outline.** This chapter is planned, not yet written. The bullets
> below are the ground it will cover.

- Plain text as a feature: files that stay readable, portable and durable long after any particular app is gone.
- When to reach for structure (headings, lists) and when to just write prose and let structure emerge later.
- Plain text and version control: why Markdown pairs naturally with git, and how that changes the way you treat drafts and revisions.
- Two lenses on the same thinking: switching between the visual and raw views to check your own structure, not just for formatting.
